8. ROLE ≠ ORGANIZATIONAL SCOPE
==================================================

This is one of the most important architectural rules.

ROLE / PERMISSION determines:

WHAT can the user do?

ORGANIZATIONAL SCOPE determines:

مدير الإدارة

Prepare one centralized organizational-scope mechanism.

Conceptually:

or equivalent.

Do NOT duplicate scope filtering separately in every page.

Later this same mechanism must be reusable for:

- Objectives
- Key Results
- Progress updates
- Approval queues
- Alerts
- Search

19. MULTIPLE ROLES PER USER
==================================================

A user may have more than one role.

Effective permissions are calculated as the UNION
of all permissions granted by those roles.

Example:

Role A:
- users.view
- cycles.view

Role B:
- reports.view

Effective user permissions:

- users.view
- cycles.view
- reports.view

If the same permission appears in multiple roles,
it appears only once in the effective permission set.

Centralize this calculation.

Do not recalculate it differently on different pages.
